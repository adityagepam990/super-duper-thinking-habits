# The Decision Notebook

* As critical thinking skills become second nature, it's easy to take them for granted — so this segment introduces a bonus tool: a decision notebook to help you track your progress.

## How to Use It

* Using a provided guide (from the exercise files), create a simple notebook — spiral or fancy, your choice — to log:

1. Decisions of consequence, big and small
2. How you arrived at each decision
3. Contextual factors, like your mood or how much sleep you got
4. Specific biases, fallacies, or judgment errors you identified and avoided using your critical thinking skills

## Why It Matters

* Keeping this record helps you see tangible improvement in the quality of your thinking over time, reinforcing the value of the skills you've built.
